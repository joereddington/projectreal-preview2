<p class="intro">Become a misinformation detective.</p>

Super Debunkers is a game that puts your misinformation-spotting skills to the test. Can you identify the tricks being used to make misleading information look convincing? Play, investigate and see if you can beat the bots, celebs and trolls.

<a class="button" href="https://super-debunkers.vercel.app/">Play Super Debunkers ↗</a>

## Bringing it into the classroom?

Use the game to start a discussion about the techniques that make misleading information convincing. Our [Spot it lessons]({{ '/spot-it.html' | relative_url }}) include slides and teaching notes on misinformation.

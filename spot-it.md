<p class="eyebrow">Misinformation · Six lessons</p>
<p class="intro">Think you can spot misinformation?</p>

Online misinformation can be convincing and difficult to spot. These six lessons use real-world examples and influencer videos to help young people understand how misinformation works, recognise common techniques and become more confident at questioning what they see online.

Designed for young people aged around 11–13, each session takes approximately 50–60 minutes. Teaching guides are available to read online; the slides include supporting notes.
